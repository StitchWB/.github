# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Report privately via the **Security → Advisories → Report a vulnerability**
button of the affected repository (GitHub private vulnerability reporting).
Do not file public issues or discuss unpatched vulnerabilities openly.

Include: affected repo and version, reproduction steps or PoC, impact
assessment, and whether you have a fix in mind.

## Scope

In scope: the client (Stitch-Manager), service plugins, the plugin
distribution server and its entitlement/signing logic, the community catalog
validator. Out of scope: abuse of target services' ToS, social engineering,
and vulnerabilities in third-party IDEs themselves.

## Response

Acknowledgement within 3 business days; triage and a fix-or-mitigation plan
within 14. Credit in the advisory unless you ask otherwise.

## Safe harbor

Research conducted in good faith on your own accounts and local installs is
considered authorized. Do not touch other users' data or the production
service beyond what is strictly necessary to demonstrate an issue.
